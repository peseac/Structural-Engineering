Laterally loaded screw, steel plate to timber

One screw in single shear between an outer steel plate and a timber member according to EN 1995-1-1:2004 + A1 + A2; the plate is checked in bearing according to EN 1993-1-8. In the simple mode you enter the plate thickness, the screw diameter and length; the other data follow from typical proportions. Switch to the detailed mode to enter your own values.
