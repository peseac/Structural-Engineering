Floor beam: choice of the cross-section

A simply supported timber floor beam under permanent and variable loads, designed according to EN 1995-1-1 and EN 1990. The page tries every common cross-section, checks bending (with lateral torsional buckling), shear and deflection for all load combinations, and recommends the lightest one that works. Select any cell of the table to see the full calculation for that section.
