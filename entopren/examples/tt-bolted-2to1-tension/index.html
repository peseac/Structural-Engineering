Check to the current EN 1995-1-1

Check to the proposed prEN 1995-1-1

Scope: bolts in double shear, load parallel to grain, one kmod for both materials (solid timber or glued laminated timber). The EN column checks the bolt group only (rope effect neglected). The prEN column also checks the net cross-section and block shear, and counts the rope effect. Not included in the prEN column yet: axial force in the bolts from the eccentricity (11.3.8.7), row and plug shear, staggered bolts. Where the prEN clause number is not shown, it still has to be added. Always verify the National Annex and the current text of the standard.
