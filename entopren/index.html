Timber design – EN 1995-1-1 compared with prEN 1995-1-1

Worked examples from the courses, each calculated twice: once with the current EN 1995-1-1 and once with the proposed prEN 1995-1-1. The aim is to prepare students for a period when both standards are in use at the same time. Each example opens as a three-column check: inputs on the left, the current EN in the middle and the proposed prEN on the right. Examples marked “In preparation” are placeholders.
