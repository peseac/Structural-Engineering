Laterally loaded screw, timber to timber

One screw in single shear between two timber members according to EN 1995-1-1:2004 + A1 + A2. In the simple mode you enter the screw diameter and length; the other screw data follow from typical proportions. Switch to the detailed mode to enter your own values.
